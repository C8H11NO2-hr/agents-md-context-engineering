# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be useful, respectful, and welcoming for developers with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and specific in feedback.
- Assume good intent, but correct problems directly.
- Keep discussions focused on the work.
- Credit ideas and sources.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Dismissive comments about identity, background, or ability.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
